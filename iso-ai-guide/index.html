Before using this guide

  • This is an independent learning tool. It is not endorsed by ISO or IEC and does not reproduce their paid standards.
  • Consult the licensed standards, relevant amendments and your chosen certification body for an actual conformity assessment.
  • ISO/IEC 27001 and 42001 certification concerns a defined management-system scope—not a guarantee that every AI product is safe or legally compliant.
  • The official ISO catalogue lists ISO/IEC 27090:2026 as published in October 2026. An older ISO explainer still describes a final draft; the published catalogue takes precedence for status.
  • The illustrative workstreams are not an official control equivalence, legal opinion, certification claim or promise of security.
  • The app sends no entered data to a server. Hosting infrastructure may still record ordinary web access logs. No analytics, external fonts or third-party scripts are included.
