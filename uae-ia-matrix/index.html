UAE Information Assurance Standard · 15 control families · mapped to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and NIST CSF 2.0. Nothing leaves your browser.

How it works

For each control family, record the implementation status, the owner, a target date and the evidence you could show an assessor. The summary, the gap list and the per-family bars update as you type, and a draft is kept in this browser until you reset it. Export JSON to keep a dated record, or CSV to work in a spreadsheet.

The matrix ships at family level. To work control by control, import the control list from your copy of the standard with Import controls (CSV); the template is in templates/. Read the framework notes for the mapping rules.
